You can delete your GenPlay account and the data associated with it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to submit a request.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app

1. Open the GenPlay app and sign in.
2. From the home feed, open the menu and tap **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account** at the bottom of the Settings screen.
4. Tap **Delete** in the confirmation dialog.

Deletion takes effect **immediately** and cannot be undone.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, email us:

**[{{ site.email.privacy }}](mailto:{{ site.email.privacy }}?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request)**

Please include:

- The **email address** associated with your GenPlay account (the Google or Apple account you signed in with).
- Your **username** on GenPlay, if you know it.
- The subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**.

We may ask for additional information to verify that you are the account owner before we process the request.

## What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Account profile (email, username, profile picture) | Deleted immediately |
| Your uploaded gameplay videos (UGC) | Removed from the Service immediately — no longer visible or accessible |
| Likes, watch history, and reports you submitted | Deleted immediately |
| Push notifications | We do not store your push token on our servers; it is managed by Firebase Cloud Messaging and becomes inactive when you delete your account or uninstall the app |
| Usage and log records linked to your account | Deleted or de-identified |

## What may be retained, and for how long

We may retain limited data after deletion where we are required or permitted to do so:

- **Legal and dispute-resolution records** — retained for the period required by applicable law.
- **Records related to a Terms of Service violation, DMCA action, or fraud prevention** — retained as necessary to enforce our terms and to prevent repeat abuse.
- **Aggregated or de-identified analytics** that can no longer be linked to you — retained indefinitely.
- **Analytics and crash data** (Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics) — retained and deleted according to Google's retention policies, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
- **Residual copies** — copies of deleted content may persist in secure backups and internal storage systems; they are not accessible through the Service.

## Timeline

- **In-app deletion** is processed **immediately** when you confirm — there is no grace period and deleted accounts cannot be recovered.
- **Email requests** are processed **within 30 days** of receipt (or of verifying your identity, if verification is needed). We will confirm by email when your account has been deleted.
