#### What is GenPlay?

GenPlay is a mobile app where you watch real gameplay videos from
players and download the games you like, straight from the clip.

#### Is GenPlay free?

Yes—GenPlay is free to download and use.

#### How do I upload gameplay videos?

Select a gameplay video from your phone in the GenPlay app, search for the
game by name (matching store titles appear), choose the right one, and
upload. Your clip is then linked to that game's store page for one-tap download.

#### How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap the report button on any video, or email [support@gameplaylabs.org](mailto:support@gameplaylabs.org).
We review reports and remove content that violates our guidelines.

#### Which platforms is GenPlay on?

GenPlay is available now on Google Play (Android). iOS is coming.
